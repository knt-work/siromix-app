# Interface

- Use Vietnamese copy and the shared blue–purple visual system, with shadowed light surfaces.
- Home shows a greeting with the optional teacher name and time of day, a search field that handles Vietnamese accents, and five tools in order: grading, disabled shuffle, history, profile/data and About. Show version and copyright in the footer.
- Provide Home navigation from the grading flow. Use the shared camera and review screens for both supported templates. Keep detailed camera guidance outside the bounded preview.
- Standard page routes slide their full width over 300 ms. A page enters from the right and exits to the right. Within the home route and history drill-down, the destination enters from the right when moving forward and from the left when going back. The exposed area uses the app's light background throughout each slide. Scan setup switches directly, and the camera and its review route use platform transitions.
