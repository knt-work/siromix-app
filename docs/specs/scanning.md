# Scanning and recognition

- NTL (Phiếu chuẩn Trắc Nghiệm - Tự Luận - NTL) is the default. It reads up to 40 ABCD responses and a three-digit code. Candidate number is unknown. [NTL geometry](../templates/abcd-40-compact-v1.md)
- QM2025 A4 reads Parts I–III from a single full sheet. The selected key controls whether the six-digit candidate number is required. Preserve leading zeros. If enabled and uncertain, require manual verification. [QM geometry](../templates/qm2025-a4-v1.md)
- Both modes use tap capture, torch and a bounded, non-scrolling camera viewport. Live framing is advisory. A red frame permits capture. The still reader independently validates the photo. After confirming a paper, the next-paper action returns to the same camera context.
- NTL tries the regular marker reader, then damaged-marker recovery, then automatic border recovery on the same still photo. Border recovery uses evidence from the outer strokes, internal markers and printed answer rings before reading bubbles. There is no four-tap or manual-corner flow.
- If the existing still alignment paths fail, both templates retry their standard marker checks on a temporary grayscale copy with locally normalized lighting. Use the original grayscale photo for mark reading and review crops. If alignment remains unreliable, request recapture.
- NTL and QM2025 Part I share the primary ABCD mark rule. A row is blank when all scores are at most 0.065. A single clear mark (score at least 0.33) is accepted if no other mark exceeds 0.065 or if it leads the next mark by at least 0.20. Two or more clear marks require teacher review.
- Both templates can also accept an isolated light ABCD mark when its score is at least 0.20, all other scores are at most 0.065, its lead is at least 0.14, and each of four interior samples shows a fill score of at least 0.16. Each template supplies sample locations based on its bubble geometry. Remaining ambiguous rows require teacher review.
- After NTL alignment recovery, check fill around the chosen bubble center using the same block correction as the main reading. NTL answers requiring review show the reason and any measured ABCD scores. These details are available in the current scan session.
- NTL can recover a low-contrast exam code when all three columns have a distinct digit supported by both center and off-center ink and at least two columns provide stronger center evidence. With exactly two clear digits, retry the unresolved column and accept a digit only if it has stronger center and outer-band evidence and leads its competitors in both the center and wider side bands.
- If an NTL code still has one unresolved digit, list matching complete keys for the current exam context first and mark them as suggestions. Require explicit teacher selection, even for a sole match. Other eligible keys remain available.
- Both exam-code readers may accept a solid mark scoring at least 0.50 when each other core scores below 0.12. NTL takes the lower of core and lateral ink evidence because it prints numerals inside the bubbles. QM2025 reads core ink because its numerals sit outside the bubbles.
- Retired templates remain decodable in saved keys and results. Only NTL and QM2025 are selectable for scanning.
- Release checks should include a normal sheet and a damaged NTL sheet on a phone when changing camera or recognition behavior. Private fixtures stay local. Existing regression coverage includes an NTL photo derived from a real damaged sheet with four corner markers removed.
